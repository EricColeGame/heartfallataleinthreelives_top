## Player Experience: Bugs, Saves, and Workarounds

For a free fan project, stability is remarkably good, but the community has documented real problems. These are player-reported, not official statements.

| Issue | Workaround |
|---|---|
| Softlock after recruiting on the Joel route | Speak to Cleo a second time and finish all her dialogue options before recruiting |
| Accidental save deletion | Restore previous versions of the Godot app_userdata folder on Windows |
| Mac gatekeeper blocking launch | Bypass Gatekeeper using the tutorial linked on the download page |
| Windows safety alert | Bypass the warning; the build is not notarized yet |
| No fullscreen option | Requested repeatedly; not confirmed as implemented |
| Older iMacs failing to launch | Developer reported pushing a compatibility fix |

The save-file recovery trick deserves a warning label. Players report success by navigating to `AppData\Roaming\Godot\app_userdata`, right-clicking the numbered folder, choosing "Restore Previous Version," and copying `data.globaldata` plus the individual save files back into place. Back up your current folder first.

Another community mystery: the Watchers' dialogue. Players cannot agree on whether it is decipherable cipher text or intentional gibberish. No confirmed translation exists.

## Content Warnings and Accessibility

Heartfall is rated teen and up, and the developer was unusually thorough about warnings. Four categories are toggleable in the settings menu: sudden noises, flashing, bright colors, and swearing.

General warnings include explosions, fantasy violence, blood, animal death, character death, trauma, injury, and suicide. Additional warnings contain spoilers and are listed in the in-game info menu and on the project's Carrd page.

If you are sensitive to flashing or loud audio spikes, toggle those settings before Session 1. The game does not warn you before the first major set piece.

## Tips for a First Playthrough

- **Use three save slots minimum.** Rotate them every session. Softlocks are patchable, but lost saves are not.
- **Talk to everyone twice.** Several events, including the Cleo flag on the Joel route, only fire on a second conversation.
- **Do not chase a specific route blind.** Finish one ending first, then replay with intent.
- **Read the info menu before starting.** Spoiler-tagged warnings are there for a reason.
- **Report bugs through the official form.** The developer patched Route 8 within days of release, so reports work.

For players weighing whether to start: the canon route alone justifies the download, and the alternate branches are where **Heartfall A Tale in Three Lives Dogwarts** curiosity pays off most, since faction-heavy scenes tend to live off the main path.

## FAQ

**Is Dogwarts actually in Heartfall A Tale in Three Lives Dogwarts?**
The store page does not confirm a dedicated Dogwarts or Red Army route. The game advertises 12+ characters and wildly diverging paths, and community comments confirm many cast members — but Ren and Martyn are not among the names players have publicly verified.

**Do I need to know 3rd Life to enjoy the game?**
No. The developer states that no prior knowledge is required, and the game introduces its island and cast from scratch.

**How long is Heartfall A Tale in Three Lives Dogwarts?**
A single route runs many hours, and the full script exceeds 400,000 words across all branches. Most players report finishing one ending before replaying for others.

**Is it free, and where can I play it?**
Yes, it is a free, not-for-profit fan project available for Windows, macOS, and Linux, with a Steam release announced. Start at the official itch.io page and check the patch notes before your first launch.
