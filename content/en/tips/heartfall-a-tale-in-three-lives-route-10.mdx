## Softlocks, Bugs, and Save Recovery

The most valuable information about **Heartfall A Tale in Three Lives route 10** may be preventative: knowing which reported issues exist so you can spot them early. Everything below is player experience reported on the official page, not an official bug list.

| Reported issue | Symptom | Workaround |
|---|---|---|
| Joel route, Session 5 | Cannot find certain recruits; paths blocked | Talk to the quest-giver a second time, exhaust all dialogue, then recruit |
| Cow heist route, Session 5 | Dialogue repeats after first death | Reload an earlier slot; talk to everyone before advancing |
| Flower valley scene | "You should check up on your friends" loop | Load a save from before the scene; multiple slots are essential |
| Flower husbands, Session 1 | Quest won't advance past the third step | Report via the official bug form; reload prior save |
| Save deletion | Clicking "no" to clearing saves still deletes them | Restore previous versions of the Godot userdata folder |

For Windows save recovery, one player documented a reliable method: navigate to `AppData\Roaming\Godot\app_userdata`, right-click the game folder, choose "Restore Previous Version," then copy `data.globaldata` and the individual save files back into place. Back up the current folder first — always.

If you hit something new, use the developer's bug report form rather than only commenting. The team has been responsive, and Patch 1.01 shipped within days of launch.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is there an official walkthrough for Heartfall A Tale in Three Lives route 10?**
No. The official page describes the branching philosophy but doesn't publish per-route steps. Route numbers circulate through community discussion, so treat any walkthrough — including this one — as player experience rather than developer documentation.

**Can I reach Heartfall A Tale in Three Lives route 10 on a first playthrough?**
Possibly, but it's inefficient. The canon route is the most natural first run, and it teaches you the game's dialogue and exploration rhythms. Most route hunters finish one ending first, then start a dedicated file with multiple saves.

**What should I do if route 10 stalls?**
Reload the oldest save before the stall, exhaust every optional conversation in the area, then advance. If the problem persists on the current patch, submit it through the official bug report form with your session number and route name.

**Do I need to know 3rd Life to enjoy route 10?**
No. The developers state plainly that no knowledge of 3rd Life is required, and the game presents its own interpretations of the characters. Route hunting works fine as a newcomer.

Route 10 isn't a puzzle with one answer — it's a branch in a very large, very passionate project. Save often, talk to everyone twice, and let the divergence surprise you.
