## Route 3 Ending Prep: Save Slots, Softlocks, and Patches

Nothing ruins a finale like losing forty minutes of progress to a script that will not advance. The game's development log shows a patch 1.01 that fixed a softlock on route 8, which is a good sign that the team responds to reports — but community reports still describe stalls elsewhere.

| Issue | Where players hit it | Workaround |
|---|---|---|
| Flower valley quest will not advance after crafting the explosives | Route 3, session 1 | Update to the latest build, reload an earlier save, and file a bug report |
| "You should check up on your friends" dialogue loop | A Pigeon Blue Lantern, session 8 | Load an older save; one player confirmed this clears it |
| Missing recruit and blocked paths | Joel route, session 5 | Re-talk to Cleo first; results vary by player |
| Save files wiped after declining the "clear saves" prompt | All routes | Restore a previous version of the Godot `app_userdata` folder |
| Route 8 softlock | Route 8 | Patched in v1.01 |

That last save-file workaround is worth memorizing. Community members found that the game stores its data in a Godot folder under your user profile, and that right-clicking the folder and choosing "Restore Previous Version" recovered wiped saves on Windows. Back up the real files before you try it, and keep manual copies of your route 3 save before the final stretch.

### Before you commit to the finale

## FAQ

**Is route 3 the Flowerhusbands route in Heartfall?**
Yes — community reports consistently identify route 3 as the Flowerhusbands branch centered on Scott and Jimmy. The game itself does not publish an official route chart, so the numbering comes from players mapping their runs.

**Why do I keep getting route 6 instead of the Heartfall A Tale in Three Lives route 3 ending?**
Player experience suggests route 6, the Scar-focused path, is the default landing spot for runs that lean into desert duo scenes early. Prioritize Scott and Jimmy in session 1 and keep a save from before the branch locks in.

**Can I switch to route 3 mid-playthrough?**
Generally no. Routes are set by early events, so the practical fix is loading an earlier save or starting a fresh slot. This is why rotating multiple saves is the most repeated advice in the community.

**Does the route 3 ending require knowledge of 3rd Life?**
No. The developer states that no prior knowledge of 3rd Life is needed, and the game is an independent fan project with its own interpretations of the characters.

**What should I do if my route 3 quest will not advance?**
Update to the latest build, reload a save from before the quest step, exhaust all dialogue with the quest giver, and submit a report through the developer's bug report form. Several players recovered stalled runs exactly this way.

Chasing the Heartfall A Tale in Three Lives route 3 ending is less about finding a secret trick and more about protecting your progress while you follow the flower valley thread to its conclusion. Save often, stay close to Scott and Jimmy, and read the content warnings before the finale lands — because when it does, it lands hard.
