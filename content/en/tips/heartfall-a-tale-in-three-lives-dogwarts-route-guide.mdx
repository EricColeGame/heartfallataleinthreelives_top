## The Martyn Encounter Tree: Route 2 vs. Route 5

The most common mistake players make is treating Martyn as a side character. He is not — he is a router. Two separate conversations decide whether you stay on Route 0 or drift into a Martyn-centric branch, and both happen before the Dogwarts battle ever starts.

| When | Your response | Where it sends you |
|---|---|---|
| Day one, village, Martyn asks how you are | Complain about Scar | Route 2 – Dog-Eat-Dog World |
| Day one, village, Martyn asks how you are | Complain, then take it back as a joke | Route 5 – Should've Aimed for the Hand |
| After the tree magic trick, forest trail | Agree to commit a crime with him | Route 5 |
| After the tree magic trick, forest trail | Turn him down | Route 0 stays intact |
| Gathering supplies, village | Refuse to accompany him | Route 0 stays intact |

In this Heartfall A Tale in Three Lives Dogwarts route guide, those two conversations are the real fork — not the battle. If you want the classic Route 0 endings, keep your answers bland, decline the cow heist with Jimmy and Scott, and never accept Martyn's crime offer. Boring choices are protective choices.

## Route 0's Fork: Take the Blow or Don't

The single most important line in any Heartfall A Tale in Three Lives Dogwarts route guide is this: when Martyn swings his axe at you and Scar during the Dogwarts fight, the answer to "do you take the blow?" decides your achievement.

| Battle moment | Choice | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| Martyn attacks you and Scar with his axe | Do not take the blow | Scar survives, the story follows the original 3rd Life arc, and you can reach **So That's How It Went** |
| Same moment | Take the blow | You die, respawn on red early, and lock into **That's Not How It Went** |

Players who take the hit "just to see what happens" without a save usually regret it, because the alternate ending kills both you and Scar later in the same battle. Save before the confrontation, then branch from there.

The good ending is long and messy: after Dogwarts falls, Scar talks BDubs over with a clock, BDubs kills BigB and Impulse, and you end up in a final duel in the desert that you win before jumping off the cliff. The alternate is shorter and grimmer — Scar survives the axe, finds you after you respawn, and you both die in the later version of the fight.

## Preparing for the Dogwarts Battle

Use this Heartfall A Tale in Three Lives Dogwarts route guide as a pre-flight checklist rather than a walkthrough. Almost every ending in the game is decided by preparation you did hours earlier.

| Prep task | Best time to do it | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Create a fresh save slot | Before every session | Routes 2, 5, 6, 7 and 9 all branch off Route 0 |
| Decline the cow heist | Day one, after the first night | Keeps the Route 0 ending available |
| Refuse Martyn's crime offer | After the tree magic trick | Stops you drifting into Route 5 |
| Save when prompted | Night of session five (Route 4) | The next morning splits A Lonely Death from A Violent End |
| Confirm Pearl's status | Route 8, during Martyn's attack | She has one life only — tell her to run |
| Deliver every fishing gift | Route 10, before sleeping | The pacifist route stalls without it |

That Pearl detail catches a lot of people. She does not get three lives like the main cast, so a single bad call during Martyn's attack removes her permanently and blocks the music box ending entirely.

## Dogwarts Beyond Route 0

The rest of this Heartfall A Tale in Three Lives Dogwarts route guide would be misleading if it stopped at Route 0, because the faction shapes several other endings in quieter ways.

In **Route 4 – Ashes and Alliances**, Dogwarts is the reason the Crastle alliance forms at all. You help Joel tame wolves, accept his cabin offer, and then spend the middle of the route as a reluctant ally of Cleo and BDubs. When the final battle approaches, the two of you are sent to recruit Etho, Scar, Jimmy and Scott — and only Jimmy sounds genuinely enthusiastic. Choosing to run away with Joel the next morning drops your reputation with Cleo and BDubs instantly and commits you to A Lonely Death.

In **Route 8 – Music is Magic**, the Dogwarts battle leaves a very specific survivor list: BDubs, Impulse, Pearl, Scar, Martyn and you. Martyn is found weakened in the village and killed there, and what happens at the Crastle afterward decides between Dal Segno al Coda, Fallen Companions and Self-Inflicted Solitude.

In **Route 10 – Any-fin is Possible**, Dogwarts is almost comic relief. Ren and Martyn stay suspicious and hostile until you fish up a guitar and hand it to Ren, at which point "Red Winter is coming" turns out to mean a guitar riff rather than an invasion.

| Route | Who is still standing after the Dogwarts battle | What it leads to |
|---|---|---|
| Route 0 (refused the blow) | BigB, BDubs, Impulse, Scar, you | So That's How It Went |
| Route 0 (took the blow) | You and Scar, briefly | That's Not How It Went |
| Route 8 | BDubs, Impulse, Pearl, Scar, Martyn, you | The music box endings |

## Mistakes That Cost You Endings

Most players searching for a Heartfall A Tale in Three Lives Dogwarts route guide run into the same handful of problems. Here are the ones worth avoiding:

- **Taking the axe hit without a save.** It is a permanent redirect, not a curiosity.
- **Complaining to Martyn "just to see."** Curiosity pulls you off Route 0 and into Route 2 or 5.
- **Accepting the cow heist.** Fun scene, but it removes the Route 0 ending from that save.
- **Overwriting the session-five save in Route 4.** That prompt exists precisely so you can branch.
- **Telling Pearl to fight.** Only do this if you are deliberately hunting Fallen Companions.
- **Ignoring content warnings.** Route 4 has extreme blood, Route 8 has body horror, and Route 11 layers horror with starvation and derealisation themes.

## FAQ

**Which choice actually splits Route 0 at Dogwarts?**
Whether you take Martyn's axe blow. Refusing it keeps Scar alive and preserves the classic ending; taking it puts you on red early and locks the alternate ending.

**Can I unlock both Route 0 endings on one save?**
No. Save in a separate slot immediately before the Dogwarts confrontation and replay from there — the game does not let you walk both paths in a single run.

**Are Route 2 and Route 5 really separate routes?**
Community reports are genuinely split on this. Internal code labels reference "Ren WhoDunnit" and "Dogwarts Deception" instead of the visible route names, so treat the numbering as a helpful convention rather than a confirmed structure.

**Does this Heartfall A Tale in Three Lives Dogwarts route guide contain spoilers?**
Yes, it references ending outcomes and survivor lists throughout. The route logic is still useful if you skim, but the tables do reveal who lives and who does not.
