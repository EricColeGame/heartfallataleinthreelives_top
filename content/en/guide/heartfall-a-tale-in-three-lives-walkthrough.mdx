## A Step-by-Step Heartfall A Tale in Three Lives Walkthrough Workflow

Here is the loop that keeps a thirty-six-ending game from turning into a chore. It assumes you want to enjoy the story first and clean up later.

1. **Run one blind playthrough.** No guide, no ending list. Just note the branch you landed in and the ending ID you received at the credits.
2. **Log it in the tracker.** Ending ID, branch, the decision you think caused it, and the save slot you can reload.
3. **Identify the nearest fork.** Reload the latest pre-decision save rather than starting over. Most endings sit a short distance from a branch point.
4. **Check the decisive condition first.** The endings guide lists the condition that picks each ID. Read it before you replay the branch, not after.
5. **Sweep the branch.** Once you are inside a branch, collect every ending attached to it before moving on. Branch-hopping is what burns people out.
6. **Retire finished branches.** Mark them done and archive the saves. A shorter active list makes the remaining endings feel reachable.

This order matters. Players who chase endings across all twelve branches at once end up with a dozen half-finished saves and no memory of which fork they were testing. Finish one branch, close it out, move on.

## Tracking Thirty-Six Endings Without Burnout

The endings guide gives you the two hardest pieces of data for free: the ending IDs and the decisive conditions. Your job is to add the human layer — where you were, what you chose, and how to get back there.

| Tracker column | Example entry | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Ending ID | The ID exactly as listed in the official endings guide | Keeps your notes matching the reference |
| Branch | Branch 4 of 12 | Groups endings so you can sweep efficiently |
| Decisive condition | The specific choice or action named in the guide | Tells you what to change on the reload |
| Save slot | Slot 4 — pre-fork | Your fastest route back |
| Status | Locked / Unlocked | Instant progress read |
| Notes | "Tried the honest answer; try the evasive one next" | Saves you from repeating a failed attempt |

Two habits do most of the work. First, record the ending ID the moment the credits start — do not trust memory an hour later. Second, when a decisive condition is vague, change exactly one variable on the reload. Changing three things at once tells you nothing about which one mattered.

On spoilers: the official endings guide is a reference, not a script. Read the condition for the ending you are actively hunting, then close it. Reading all thirty-six at once flattens the experience of a 400,000-word story into a checklist.
