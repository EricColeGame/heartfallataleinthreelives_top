**Heartfall A Tale in Three Lives routes** are the reason this fan-made visual novel has consumed so many players' weekends. One run casts Grian as a detective; the next turns him into an alliance-breaking schemer; a third drags him into secrets the story clearly wanted left buried. If you have ever finished a playthrough and wondered why someone else's story looked nothing like yours, the answer is almost always route selection.

This guide explains how **Heartfall A Tale in Three Lives routes** are structured, which branches the community has documented so far, and how to keep a bad quest step or an unlucky save from ending your run early.

## What Makes These Routes So Different From Other Visual Novels

*Heartfall — A Tale in Three Lives* is a free, downloadable fangame by developer Kazehita, built for Windows, macOS, and Linux and also distributed through Steam. It follows Grian as he wakes on a mysterious island, meets a cast of more than a dozen characters, and stumbles through "fatal-but-temporary shenanigans" that reshuffle the story depending on what you do.

The scale is genuinely unusual for a fan project: the team advertises 400,000+ words of dialogue, 450+ cut-in artworks, and 50+ original music tracks, produced by roughly seventy collaborators. That volume is exactly why the branching feels so wide — there is real content behind the paths you never take.

A few things worth knowing before you plan a route:

- No prior knowledge of 3rd Life is required, though veterans will recognize plenty of nods.
- The game is rated teen and up, with content warnings covering explosions, fantasy violence, blood, animal death, character death, trauma, injury, and suicide.
- Sudden noises, flashing, bright colors, and swearing can all be toggled in the settings menu.
- It is an independent, not-for-profit fan project, unaffiliated with Grian or the 3rd Life SMP cast.

You can grab the current build from the [official Heartfall itch.io page](https://kazehita.itch.io/heartfall-a-tale-in-three-lives), which also lists patch notes and the bug report form.

## How a Route Plays Out: Sessions, Choices, and Branch Points

Routes in Heartfall are not selected from a menu. They emerge from a stack of small systems, and understanding each one makes your choices far more predictable.

| System | How it works | Why it matters for routes |
|---|---|---|
| Sessions | Chapters that echo the pacing of the original Life Series | Major branch points tend to sit at session boundaries |
| Dialogue choices | Conversation options with 12+ characters | Small replies can flag scenes many sessions later |
| Quest chains | Multi-step objectives, such as opening the flower valley | Step order sometimes decides whether a route continues |
| Deaths | Fatal but temporary, per the developer's description | Some branches continue after a death scene rather than ending |
| Save slots | Manual saves managed by the player | Your only real protection against a softlock |

The practical takeaway: treat every session break as a checkpoint. Most community bug reports involve a scene that fails to trigger *after* a specific conversation, which means the damage is usually done several minutes before you notice it.

## Practical Tips for Reaching a Specific Route

If you are chasing one particular branch — say the Flower Husbands path rather than the Scar route — brute-forcing choices rarely works. Use this sequence instead.

1. **Finish the canon route first.** It teaches you the map layout, the cast, and where each session ends, which makes later runs dramatically faster.
2. **Save in every slot, every session.** Community members who lost progress consistently say the same thing: multiple slots saved them, a single slot did not.
3. **Exhaust conversations before triggering quests.** One widely shared fix for the Joel route softlock involves speaking with Cleo a second time and reading all her dialogue options before you start recruiting.
4. **Follow the order the game nudges you toward.** A player report describes a softlock when gathering wood first while building the base in Session 1 of the Joel route.
5. **Update before you start a new run.** Patch 1.01 specifically addressed a softlock on Route 8, and later fixes have targeted Mac compatibility.
6. **Keep a short written log.** Note which characters you spoke to and which quest steps you completed; route bugs are far easier to diagnose when you can point to the exact step.

A quick planning table can help you decide what to replay:

| Goal | Recommended starting point | Time investment |
|---|---|---|
| Understand the story | Canon route | Full playthrough |
| See maximum emotional payoff | Desert Duo route | Medium |
| Try a lighter, comedic branch | Cow Heist route | Medium |
| Chase lore and secrets | Watcher-related branch | Variable |
| Test a specific alliance | Route 3 or Route 6 | Short-to-medium |

## Softlocks and Route Bugs: What to Watch For

The single biggest threat to your route progress is not a hard choice — it is a scene that refuses to fire. Community reports follow a recognizable pattern.

| Reported issue | Where it appears | Player workaround |
|---|---|---|
| Dialogue repeats before Scar's death scene | Desert Duo route | Reload an earlier save from a different slot |
| Cannot progress the flower valley quest | Flower Husbands route, Session 1 | Re-craft and re-check quest steps; report via the official form |
| Scar or Etho missing while recruiting | Joel route, Session 5 | Talk to Cleo twice before recruiting, then retry |
| Blocked exit with "check up on your friends" message | "A Pigeon Blue Lantern," Session 8 | Load an older save |
| Save files wiped by the clear-save prompt | Any route | Restore previous versions of the Godot app data folder on Windows |

That last one deserves emphasis: on Windows, save data lives in the Godot `app_userdata` folder, and players have recovered wiped files using the system's "Restore Previous Version" feature. Back up that folder before you experiment with deleting saves.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Heartfall A Tale in Three Lives Routes

**How many routes does Heartfall actually have?**
There is no official public count. The developer describes "many story routes" that diverge wildly, while players have documented at least eight numbered branches plus several named ones. Expect the list to grow as patches and updates arrive.

**Do I need to watch 3rd Life before playing?**
No. The game explicitly states that no knowledge of 3rd Life is required, and the cast's interpretations are the team's own rather than canon to the SMP.

**Why do I keep getting the Scar route when I want Route 3?**
Community reports suggest early dialogue choices decide this branch, and many players hit the Scar path by accident. Save before the first major session break so you can retest your choices without replaying everything.

**Are the Heartfall A Tale in Three Lives routes bug-free now?**
Not entirely. Version 1.01 fixed a Route 8 softlock, but players still report progression blocks on several branches. Keep multiple save slots, stay on the latest patch, and send reproducible bugs to the developer's bug report form so they can be addressed.
