## Save-Slot Strategy for a Clean Canon Run

Your Heartfall A Tale in Three Lives canon route is only as safe as your saves. A single overwritten slot is the most common way players lose a branch they meant to revisit. Use rotating saves, and name them with enough detail that future-you can understand them without opening each file.

| Save slot | When to use it | Suggested label | Keep or overwrite? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Slot 1 | Before the first major quest | “Opening — pre-quest” | Keep until the finale |
| Slot 2 | After a major choice but before the next route section | “Midgame — branch A” | Keep if the branch feels canon-relevant |
| Slot 3 | Immediately before Route 8 | “Pre-Route 8 — patched” | Keep until you clear the section |
| Slot 4 | Before the final point of no return | “Pre-finale — canon check” | Keep permanently |
| Slot 5 | Experimental branch | “Test — do not overwrite” | Overwrite freely after testing |

Three habits make this work. First, save before a quest, not only after it. Second, keep one “clean” save that you never touch unless you are starting a new branch. Third, write down the patch version next to your route notes. If a later update changes progression, you will know whether your save predates the fix.

Inventory checks are also worth a glance. Patch 1.01 specifically addresses the “Wood Block” item not leaving the inventory as intended in an early section. If you notice an item that should have been removed, check your version and reload an earlier save if the oddity blocks progress. Do not assume every strange inventory state is a bug, but do treat unexplained leftovers as a reason to pause and verify.

## Common Mistakes That Derail a Canon Route Run

Most broken Heartfall A Tale in Three Lives canon route runs do not fail because of one dramatic wrong choice. They fail because of small housekeeping mistakes that remove the ability to recover. In player experience, the biggest regrets tend to involve overwritten saves, unpatched versions, and notes that were too vague to be useful.

| Mistake | What it looks like | Better fix |
|---|---|---|
| One-save syndrome | You overwrite the same slot after every scene | Rotate at least three slots and keep a pre-finale save |
| Skipping the patch | You resume an old download and hit a known softlock | Re-download Patch 1.01 and confirm saves transferred |
| Vague notes | “Chose the nice option” tells you nothing later | Record the quest name, character reaction, and immediate result |
| Full-guide dependence | You follow a script and lose the sense of discovery | Use checkpoints only at permanent choices or suspected bugs |
| Ignoring quest names | You cannot tell “Music is Magic” from a generic side quest later | Use chapter and quest titles as save labels |

Community reports often focus on the Route 8 softlock because it is the most visible progression problem. The developer’s patch notes confirm that the fix is real, but they also show why version checks matter: a route that feels “broken” may simply be running on an older download. Before you restart, verify your patch, reload a save from before the stuck quest, and test whether progression continues.

A final mistake is treating canon as a race. If you rush, you may miss the small character reactions that tell you which branch the story is prioritizing. Slow down at dialogue-heavy scenes, especially around quests that begin with tension between characters. Those moments often carry more route signal than a dramatic binary choice.

## FAQ

### Is there an official Heartfall A Tale in Three Lives canon route?

The developer patch notes available at the time of writing do not label one route as canon. Players use the term to describe the route that feels most central to the story’s three-lives premise. Treat it as a planning framework, not an official mode, and keep saves so you can compare branches.

### Do I need to restart after Patch 1.01?

Not necessarily. The developer states that save files transfer automatically after you re-download the game. If you were stuck on the patched softlock, load a save from before the stuck quest and verify that progression continues. If you are already past it, keep playing but maintain backups.

### Can I recover a missed canon choice without a new save?

It depends on how recently you saved and whether you kept a pre-choice slot. If you only have one save after the choice, you may need to replay from an earlier point. This is why rotating saves before major quests is the most reliable canon-route habit.

### How many saves should I keep for a canon route?

At least three active saves are a good baseline: one before the current quest, one after the last major choice, and one reserved for the pre-finale checkpoint. Add a fourth slot for Route 8 or any section named in patch notes. More saves cost little and protect hours of progress.
